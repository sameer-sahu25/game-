1. Purpose
This document defines the operational rules that gameplay systems must enforce. Numerical balance values such as final damage, cooldowns, player capacity and match duration remain configurable/open decisions unless explicitly established by the source plan.
2. Core Fair-Play Rules
The server is authoritative for damage, combat outcomes, currency, rewards and kill credit.
Players may send action intent, but may not declare damage, target validity, currency, match result or reward amount.
Attacks must obey configured cooldowns, ranges, attack phases and target eligibility.
Major attacks must have readable startup/telegraphing or another meaningful response window.
Long combos must not become unavoidable defeats.
Unauthorized speed changes, teleportation or invulnerability are prohibited.
Friendly fire is disabled by default in team modes.
Competitive character attributes must remain balanced; progression should not create unavoidable combat advantages.
Exploit attempts and invalid remote requests are rejected server-side.

4. Combo Rules
Support configurable three-hit and five-hit chains.
Each combo uses defined timing windows.
Combo state resets when its reset conditions occur.
Longer combos may use damage scaling.
Finishing attacks may apply stronger knockback.
Air-combo behavior is optional/configurable.
Combo hit confirmation must come from validated combat state.
5. Ability Rules
Each ability definition shall contain: name, description, input, activation conditions, cooldown, optional energy cost, range, damage, area of effect, startup, active duration, recovery, animation, sound, VFX, server validation and counterplay.
6. Ultimate Rules
Ultimate meter increases from legitimate combat activity.
Meter progress is visible to the player.
Activation requires sufficient meter.
Ultimate activation has distinctive animation/audio presentation.
Ultimates must have clear telegraphing and fair hit detection.
Damage/duration must be balanced through configurable data.
Ultimates must not be designed as unavoidable attacks.
7. Free-for-All Rules
Players compete individually.
Each eligible player has an individual score.
The match uses a configurable timer.
Kills contribute to the configured scoring system.
At match end, the server calculates results from authoritative match data.
Eligible rewards are distributed once.
Players receive a results screen and may return to the lobby or queue again.
8. 1v1 Duel Rules
Two players compete with equal starting conditions.
The mode uses configurable rounds.
Match victory is based on round wins.
Ranked matchmaking is a later-development feature unless separately enabled.
9. 2v2 Team Battle Rules
Two teams of two players compete.
Team score determines the configured victory condition.
Team identity is shown through UI/visual markers.
Friendly fire is disabled by default.
10. Training Grounds Rules
No competitive scoring.
Practice dummies may be stationary, moving, blocking, dodging, combo-focused or damage-test focused.
Damage and useful training feedback are displayed.
Players may reset dummies and, where enabled, reset ability cooldowns.
Training rewards remain separate from competitive rewards unless explicitly balanced and validated.
11. Survival Rules
Players fight increasing waves of AI opponents.
Difficulty increases through configured wave progression.
Rewards are distributed according to validated wave outcomes.
AI uses readable attacks and consistent gameplay rules.
Boss encounters are a future extension.
12. Match Lifecycle Rules
Waiting for eligible players.
Queue registration and eligibility validation.
Matchmaking and arena selection.
Spawn/teleport and character initialization.
Pre-match countdown.
Active combat.
End-condition evaluation.
Final result calculation.
Idempotent reward distribution.
Results display.
Return to lobby or next queue.
13. Disconnect / Failure Rules
A player leaving must not strand remaining players in an invalid match state.
Unexpected deaths, late arrivals and player-count changes must be handled by the match manager.
Match rewards must not be duplicated because of reconnects or repeated completion events.
Server shutdown must follow safe persistence and match cleanup procedures.
Failed DataStore loads must not be replaced with defaults and then saved over existing progress.
14. Progression & Reward Rules
Track level, experience, kills, deaths, wins, losses, matches, streaks, achievements, owned fighters and cosmetics.
Experience may come from completed matches, victories and configured achievements/objectives.
Battle Coins may come from match participation, victories, legitimate kills, daily rewards and achievements.
All important balance changes and item grants are server-authoritative.
Rewards must be protected against duplicate processing.
Unlocks should provide meaningful progression without selling unavoidable competitive advantages.

16. Map Rules
Spawn positions must be distributed fairly.
Arena boundaries must be clearly defined.
Geometry must avoid unintended collision traps.
Hazards, where used, must be clearly telegraphed with enough reaction time.
Additional arenas must follow a common arena template, spawn validation and performance budget.
The Eternal Arena is the baseline production map for the MVP.
17. UI/Information Rules
Players must be able to understand health, score, timer, cooldowns and ultimate progression.
Ability availability and cooldown state must be visually clear.
Match completion must clearly communicate victory/defeat and rewards.
Mobile combat buttons must remain reachable without unnecessarily obstructing the gameplay view.
Reduced camera shake/effects options should be respected.
