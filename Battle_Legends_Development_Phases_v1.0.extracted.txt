1. Development Strategy
The project shall be developed incrementally. Every phase must leave the project runnable, and a phase is not complete merely because scripts exist; implementation must be tested in the appropriate environment.
2. MVP Gate
The MVP gate is the first major release checkpoint: lobby, one arena, two fighters, basic combat, one special per fighter, FFA match loop, HUD/session stats and PC/mobile controls. The exit condition is an end-to-end playable loop without critical errors.

5. Phase Testing Standard
Functional test: feature performs its intended action.
Integration test: feature works with dependent systems.
Multiplayer test: server/client behavior remains authoritative and consistent.
Failure test: disconnects, invalid requests and unexpected states are handled safely where relevant.
Device test: PC and touch behavior are checked for MVP.
Performance test: expensive geometry/effects/scripts are profiled before release.
Evidence: record tested version, scenario, result, issue and resolution.
