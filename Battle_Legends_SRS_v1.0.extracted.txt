1. Purpose
This SRS translates the Battle Legends product plan into implementable software requirements. It defines system behavior, functional requirements, non-functional requirements, interfaces, data, security, acceptance criteria and dependencies. It preserves the source plan's MVP-first approach.
2. Product Scope
Battle Legends is a stylized third-person multiplayer Roblox fighting game built around readable combat, original fighters, arena battles, progression and repeatable matchmaking.
2.1 MVP Scope
3D social lobby.
The Eternal Arena.
At least two original playable fighters.
Light/basic attacks, heavy attacks, defense and dodge.
One special ability per MVP fighter.
Free-for-all match lifecycle.
Battle HUD, results and session statistics.
PC and mobile/touch controls.
Server-authoritative combat and basic exploit protection.
2.2 Release 1 Scope
Eight original fighters.
Three to five arenas as production capacity allows.
Progression, Battle Coins, unlocks and cosmetics.
DataStore persistence.
Training mode, leaderboards and core settings.
Required animation, effects, audio and tutorial content.
2.3 Post-MVP / Later Scope
1v1 Duel, 2v2 Team Battle and Survival.
AI survival opponents.
Ranked matchmaking.
Events, expanded social features and additional content.

5. Combat System Requirements
Combat must feel responsive while remaining server authoritative.
Client sends intent only; it does not declare damage, valid targets, currency or match outcomes.
Attack definitions shall include cooldown, range, startup, active/recovery timing, damage, knockback, animation, audio, VFX and counterplay data.
Combo systems shall support configurable three-hit/five-hit chains, timing windows, reset conditions and damage scaling.
Long combos must not become unavoidable defeats.
Major attacks and ultimates require clear telegraphing and reaction opportunities.
Temporary invulnerability, movement restrictions and crowd-control effects must be explicitly configured rather than implicit.

11. Security Requirements
Never trust client-provided damage values.
Never trust client-provided currency balances or reward claims.
Validate equipped fighter and ownership server-side.
Validate cooldowns and attack phases server-side.
Validate target eligibility and range.
Rate-limit RemoteEvents and reject malformed/excessive requests.
Compute kill attribution and rewards on the server.
Prevent unauthorized teleportation, speed changes and invulnerability.
Guard match completion and rewards against duplicate processing.

New fighters/maps/abilities/rewards should primarily use reusable modules and configuration.

Review current Roblox safety, privacy, asset, monetization and publication requirements before release.
13. Asset Dependencies
3D fighters, environment models, props and UI assets must be created or imported with valid permissions.
Animations require valid uploaded animation assets/IDs before production use.
Audio must be original, licensed or otherwise Roblox-permitted.
The project must explicitly track assets that are implemented, tested, missing, externally dependent or planned.
14. Acceptance Criteria
Project opens and initializes without critical errors.
Lobby and one complete arena load correctly.
At least two original fighters can be selected, spawned and controlled.
PC and mobile users can move, attack and use at least one special per fighter.
Server validates attacks, damage, cooldowns and kill attribution.
A complete FFA match starts, runs, ends and displays results.
Players can respawn or return to the lobby according to configured rules.
HUD displays accurate health, match status and session statistics.
Invalid/excessive remote requests are rejected.
Core UI actions work, and unavailable features are clearly identified.
Multi-client Studio testing is completed and limitations recorded.
